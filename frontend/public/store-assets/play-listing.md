Nice2Meechu is the video-first way to meet people in your town.

No endless swiping through filtered photos. No catfishing. Just real people,
saying hi in their own voice — 7 seconds at a time.

HOW IT WORKS
• Record one 7-second video intro: your face, your voice, your city
• Watch short intros from real people near you
• Stay anonymous — codenames only, until you both say "nice2meechu"
• Match mutually and chat instantly, with built-in translation

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• Hyperlocal: your feed starts in YOUR town, not a 100-mile radius
• Video-first: hear their voice before you ever share your name
• Honest: real videos only — AI demo profiles are always clearly labeled
• Private: your exact location is never shown to anyone

EXPLORE THE WORLD

how people say hello around the globe.
